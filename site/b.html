Mash by recipe.
Not by guesswork.

BrewberryPi turns a Raspberry Pi into the brain of your mash kettle. It heats, holds every rest to the minute, runs the pump, and calls you when the malt goes in. You watch it all from your phone.

Flash and brew

Write the image with Raspberry Pi Imager. The app starts on its own at every boot.

Updates from the browser

New versions install from the device page. They are signature-checked, and the previous one stays for rollback.

Built to fail safe

A read-only system that survives power cuts, and outputs that go off on every stop, crash or hang.

Recipes

A recipe is a list of steps.
BrewberryPi works through it.

Heat up, rest, mash in, iodine test, lautering, boil. A recipe is a small JSON file, and each step switches the heater and pump on its own or waits for you. Here is a real one: a step mash for a wheat beer.

    The case

    Print the box.
    Plug in the kettle.

    A 3D-printable enclosure for the Pi, the relay board and the power parts, with switches at the front and sockets for heater and pump at the back.

    Drag to turn
    Front
    Switches for main power, heater and pump
    Back
    Mains inlet with fuse, sockets for heater and pump, jack for the temperature probe
    Inside
    Raspberry Pi 3, 4 or 5, relay board and USB-C power supply; a divider keeps mains and low voltage apart
    Print
    4 parts, each within 200 mm, on any FDM printer; brass heat-set inserts

    Mains voltage inside. A printed case is not mains insulation on its own: have the wiring done or checked by a qualified electrician.

    Get started

    From parts to first brew
    in three steps.

    1. 1

      Print and wire

      Print the case and fit the Pi, the relay board, the DS18B20 probe and the buzzer. The wiring guide shows every pin.

      STL files → Wiring guide →

    2. 2

      Flash the image

      Write BrewberryPi OS to an SD card with Raspberry Pi Imager; the app is included. Set a host name and your Wi-Fi, or set Wi-Fi up later through its own hotspot.

      Flashing guide →

    3. 3

      Open it and brew

      Open http://brewberrypi.local/ on your phone (use the host name you chose), load a recipe and press start.

      User manual →
