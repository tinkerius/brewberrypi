Terms of use

The rules for using the BrewberryPi website, the recipe editor, the software and the hardware designs.

Last changed 6 November 2026

BrewberryPi is a free, open-source hobby project: a Raspberry Pi controller for a home-brewing mash kettle, its operating-system image, a 3D-printable case, this website and the recipe editor at recipes.brewberrypi.org. By using them you agree to these terms.

The software and the case files are published on GitHub under the licences stated in each repository. Those licences govern their use, copying and changes.

Provided as is

Everything is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, to the extent permitted by law. BrewberryPi switches a mains-powered heater. You are responsible for building, wiring and operating your equipment safely; have the mains wiring done or checked by a qualified electrician, and never leave a running brew unattended. The recipe editor checks that a recipe has the format the BrewberryPi app reads, not that it is a good or safe way to brew.

Your data

Your recipes belong to you. In the recipe editor they are stored only in your browser, or, when you sign in with Google, in your own Google Drive. Keep copies of recipes you care about: browser storage can be cleared, and BrewberryPi cannot restore lost recipes. See the privacy policy.
