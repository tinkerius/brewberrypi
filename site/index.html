Mash by recipe.
Not by guesswork.

BrewberryPi turns a Raspberry Pi into the brain of your mash kettle. It heats, holds every rest to the minute, runs the pump, and calls you when the malt goes in. You watch it all from your phone.

Recipes

A recipe is a list of steps.
BrewberryPi works through it.

Heat up, rest, mash in, iodine test, lautering, boil. A recipe is a small JSON file, and each step switches the heater and pump on its own or waits for you. Here is a real one: a step mash for a wheat beer.

    Components

    Three parts.
    One brewing machine.

    The app, the operating system and the case are separate open projects. Take all three, or just the parts you need.

    brewberrypi-app

    The app

    The brewing engine and the web UI. It works through the recipe, controls the temperature and keeps every screen in sync.

    • Python back end, Preact web UI
    • REST and server-sent events
    • Simulation mode with kettle physics
    • Signed container image

    brewberrypi-os

    The OS

    A ready-made image for the Raspberry Pi. Flash it, boot it, brew. It runs the app as a container and keeps it safe.

    • Raspberry Pi OS Trixie, Pi 3 · 4 · 5
    • Read-only root, survives power cuts
    • Signed app updates, rollback, safe mode
    • Device settings in the browser

    brewberrypi-case

    The case

    A 3D-printable enclosure for the Pi, the relay board and the power parts. A divider keeps the mains side apart from the low-voltage side.

    • 4 parts, printable on any FDM printer
    • Switches for main power, heater and pump
    • Mains inlet and sockets at the back
    • Designed from a written spec in OpenSCAD

    Get started

    From parts to first brew
    in three steps.

    1. 1

      Print and wire

      Print the case and fit the Pi, the relay board, the DS18B20 probe and the buzzer. The wiring guide shows every pin.

      STL files → Wiring guide →

    2. 2

      Flash the OS

      Write BrewberryPi OS to an SD card with Raspberry Pi Imager. Set a host name and your Wi-Fi, or set Wi-Fi up later through its own hotspot.

      Flashing guide →

    3. 3

      Open it and brew

      Open http://brewberrypi.local/ on your phone (use the host name you chose), load a recipe and press start.

      User manual →

    BrewberryPi switches a mains-powered heater. A printed case is not mains insulation on its own. Have the mains wiring done or checked by a qualified electrician.
