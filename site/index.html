BrewberryPiThe DIY Homebrewing Controller for Raspberry Pi

Your homebrewing companion: Let a Raspberry Pi follow your recipe and control your electric mash kettle.

The components→ Raspberry Pi, relay board and probe at your kettleMore info → + The software→ App and OS for the Raspberry Pi, used from any browserMore info → + The case→ 3D-printable enclosure for the Pi, relays and mains partsMore info →

How it works

From the kettle
to your phone.

The Pi reads the temperature in the mash and switches the electric heater and the pump/stirrer through a relay board. You follow the brew on your computer or phone.

Get started

From parts to first brew
in three steps.

  1. 1

    Print, assemble and wire

    Print the case, assemble the Pi, the relay board and the power parts in it, and wire the probe and the buzzer. The wiring guide shows every pin.

    The case → Wiring guide →

  2. 2

    Flash the image

    Write BrewberryPi OS to an SD card with Raspberry Pi Imager; the app is included. Set a host name and your Wi-Fi, or set Wi-Fi up later through its own hotspot.

    Flashing guide →

  3. http://brewberrypi.local 3

    Open it and brew

    Open http://brewberrypi.local/ on your phone (use the host name you chose), load a recipe and press start.

    User manual →

BrewberryPi switches a mains-powered heater. A printed case is not mains insulation on its own. Have the mains wiring done or checked by a qualified electrician.
