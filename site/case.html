BrewberryPi / Case

The case

A 3D-printable enclosure for the Pi, the relay board and the power parts: switches at the front, sockets for heater and pump at the back, and a divider between mains and low voltage.

brewberrypi-case · 4 parts
Drag to turn
Front
Switches for main power, heater and pump
Back
Mains inlet with fuse, sockets for heater and pump, jack for the temperature probe
Inside
Raspberry Pi 3, 4 or 5, relay board and USB-C power supply; a divider keeps mains and low voltage apart
Print
4 parts, each within 200 mm, on any FDM printer; brass heat-set inserts

Mains voltage inside. A printed case is not mains insulation on its own: have the wiring done or checked by a qualified electrician.

Printing

Four parts.
One colour.

Printable on any FDM printer. The model holds no printer or slicer settings: choose those in your slicer.

4 parts

Body, divider, top lid and bottom lid. Each fits within 200 × 200 × 200 mm.

Heat-set inserts

Brass inserts and screws: M2.5 for the boards, M3 for everything else.

Raised labels

MAIN, HEATER, PUMP and the logo stand out from the walls; colour them with a paint pen.

Status: the model is done and checked; the first test print is still open.
