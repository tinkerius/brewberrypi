BrewberryPi / Components

The components

The Pi reads the temperature in the mash and switches the electric heater and the pump/stirrer through a relay board. You follow the brew on your computer or phone.

How it works

From the kettle
to your phone.

Pins, wiring and the full parts list: Wiring guide →

The heater and the pump run on mains power. Have the mains wiring done or checked by a qualified electrician, with a residual-current device (RCD) in the circuit.
